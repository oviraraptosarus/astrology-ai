# Marriage (Kalatrasthana) Analysis Protocol

## 1. PRIMARY FACTORS
- **7th House (D1)**: The core house of partnership and spouse.
- **7th Lord (D1)**: Its placement, dignity, and conjunctions.
- **Planets in 7th (D1)**: Planets occupying the 7th house.
- **Darakaraka (DK)**: The Jaimini significator for spouse.
- **Venus (for men) & Jupiter (for women)**: Natural significators of marriage.

## 2. SECONDARY FACTORS
- **Upapada Lagna (UL)**: Shows the nature of the marriage, the family of the spouse, and the longevity of the union.
- **2nd House & 8th House**: Family life (2nd) and longevity of the marriage/joint assets (8th).
- **D9 (Navamsha)**: The absolute primary divisional chart for marriage. Assess D9 Lagna, 7th house of D9, and their lords.

## 3. CONFIRMATION FACTORS
- **Kuja Dosha (Manglik)**: Mars in 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th from Lagna, Moon, or Venus.
- **Navamsha Dispositors**: The strength of the lords of the D1 7th house and Venus inside the D9 chart.

## 4. TIMING FACTORS
- **Vimshottari Dasha**: Dasha of the 7th lord, planets in 7th, Venus, or Darakaraka.
- **Chara Dasha**: Dasha of signs containing or aspecting the Darakaraka or the 7th house.
- **Transits**: Jupiter or Saturn transiting over the 7th house, 7th lord, or natal Venus.

## 5. OPTIONAL / ADVANCED METHODS
- **Navamsha Tulya Rashi**: Superimposing D9 planets onto the D1 chart to assess deep marital destiny.
- **Ashtakoota Milan**: Compatibility matching if a second chart is provided.
