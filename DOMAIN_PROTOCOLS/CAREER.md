# Career (Karmasthana) Analysis Protocol

## 1. PRIMARY FACTORS
- **10th House (D1)**: The core house of career, karma, and status.
- **10th Lord (D1)**: Its placement, dignity, and conjunctions.
- **Planets in 10th (D1)**: Planets occupying the 10th house.
- **Amatyakaraka (AmK)**: The Jaimini significator for career and wealth.
- **Sun & Mercury**: Natural significators of profession, authority, and commerce.

## 2. SECONDARY FACTORS
- **Lagna & Lagna Lord**: Defines the self who performs the karma.
- **6th House & Lord**: Service, daily work, competition.
- **2nd House & 11th House**: Income and gains derived from career.
- **D10 (Dashamsha)**: The primary divisional chart for career. Assess D10 Lagna, 10th house of D10, and their lords.

## 3. CONFIRMATION FACTORS
- **Arudha Lagna (AL) 10th House**: How the public perceives the native's career.
- **Shadbala**: Specifically the strength of the 10th lord and Sun.
- **Ashtakavarga**: BAV points of the 10th house and 10th lord's sign.

## 4. TIMING FACTORS
- **Vimshottari Dasha**: Dasha of the 10th lord, planets in 10th, or AmK.
- **Chara Dasha**: Dasha of signs aspecting the AmK or the 10th house.
- **Transits**: Saturn or Jupiter transiting over the 10th house, 10th lord, or natal Sun.

## 5. OPTIONAL / ADVANCED METHODS
- **Karakamsa (KL) 10th House**: Soul's inclination towards a profession.
- **Argala on 10th House**: Planetary interventions that force career changes.
