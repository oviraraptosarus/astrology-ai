# Jyotisha Knowledge Taxonomy

## 1. Siddhanta (Astronomical Foundation)
- Graha Gati (Planetary Motion)
- Ayanamsa (Precession of Equinoxes)
- Eclipses

## 2. Hora (Natal Prediction)
### 2.1 Core Components
- **Grahas (Planets)**: Naisargika Karakas, Exaltation/Debilitation, Moolatrikona.
- **Rashis (Signs)**: Modality, Element, Gender, Lordship.
- **Bhavas (Houses)**: Kendras, Trikonas, Dusthanas, Upachayas, Marakas.

### 2.2 Strengths & Conditions (Balas & Avasthas)
- **Shadbala**: Sthana, Dig, Kala, Cheshta, Naisargika, Drik.
- **Bhava Bala**: House strength.
- **Vimsopaka Bala**: 20-point dignity score across Vargas.
- **Avasthas**: Baladi, Jagradadi, Lajjitadi.

### 2.3 Relational Mechanics
- **Drishti**: Graha Drishti (Parashari), Rashi Drishti (Jaimini).
- **Yogas**: Raja, Dhana, Daridra, Arishta, Nabhasa, Pancha Mahapurusha.
- **Sambandha**: Mutual reception, aspect, conjunction.

### 2.4 Divisional Charts (Vargas)
- D1 (Rashi) - Physical body, general life.
- D2 (Hora) - Wealth.
- D3 (Dreshkana) - Siblings, courage.
- D4 (Chaturthamsha) - Property, destiny.
- D7 (Saptamsha) - Children, legacy.
- D9 (Navamsha) - Marriage, dharma, hidden strength.
- D10 (Dashamsha) - Career, status, karma.
- D12 (Dwadashamsha) - Parents, lineage.
- D16 (Shodashamsha) - Vehicles, happiness.
- D20 (Vimshamsha) - Spirituality.
- D24 (Chaturvimshamsha) - Education, learning.
- D27 (Saptavimshamsha) - Strengths/Weaknesses.
- D30 (Trishamsha) - Misfortunes, diseases.
- D40 (Khavedamsha) - Auspicious/Inauspicious effects.
- D45 (Akshavedamsha) - General indications.
- D60 (Shashtiamsha) - Past karma, microscopic details.

### 2.5 Constellational (Nakshatras)
- 27 Nakshatras
- Padas (Quarters)
- Nakshatra Lords & Deities

### 2.6 Timing (Dashas & Transits)
- **Vimshottari Dasha**: 120-year Nakshatra-based system.
- **Conditional Dashas**: Ashtottari, Yogini, Shodashottari.
- **Transits (Gochara)**: Sade Sati, Jupiter transit, Ashtakavarga transit rules.

## 3. Jaimini System
- **Chara Karakas**: Atmakaraka, Amatyakaraka, Bhratrukaraka, Matrukaraka, Putrakaraka, Gnatikaraka, Darakaraka.
- **Arudhas**: Arudha Lagna (AL), Upapada Lagna (UL), Bhava Arudhas.
- **Karakamsa**: Atmakaraka's position in D9.
- **Argala**: Planetary intervention.
- **Jaimini Yogas**: Distinct from Parashari.
- **Jaimini Timing**: Chara Dasha, Mandook Dasha, Narayana Dasha.

## 4. Ashtakavarga
- Bhinna Ashtakavarga (BAV)
- Sarvashtakavarga (SAV)
- Prasthara Ashtakavarga (PAV)
- Reductions (Trikona, Ekadhipatya)
- Transit Application

## 5. Prashna (Horary)
- Prashna Lagna
- Karya Bhava & Karya Lord
- Tajaka Aspects in Prashna
- Omens (Nimittas)

## 6. Muhurta (Electional)
- Panchanga: Tithi, Vara, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana.
- Rahu Kalam, Yamaganda, Gulika.
- Auspicious Yogas (Amrita Siddhi, Sarvartha Siddhi).

## 7. Specialized & Remedial
- **Tajaka**: Varshaphala (Solar Return), Tajaka Yogas, Muntha.
- **Remedies (Upayas)**: Mantras, Gemstones (Ratnas), Yantras, Danas, Vratas.
- **Ayush (Longevity)**: Marakas, Badhakas, Shoola Dasha.
