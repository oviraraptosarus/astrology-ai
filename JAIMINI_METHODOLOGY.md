# Jaimini Methodology

The Jaimini tradition differs significantly from the Parashari system. It is vital to never mix Parashari rules (like Graha Drishti) with Jaimini principles without explicit methodological boundaries.

## 1. Principles
- **Rashi Drishti**: Signs aspect signs (not planets aspecting planets).
  - Cardinal signs (Chara) aspect Fixed signs (Sthira), except the adjacent one.
  - Fixed signs aspect Cardinal signs, except the adjacent one.
  - Dual signs (Dwisvabhava) aspect other Dual signs.
- **Chara Karakas**: 7 or 8 planets representing life roles based on their degrees (excluding Rahu/Ketu for 7, including Rahu for 8).
  - Atmakaraka (AK): Self, soul, King.
  - Amatyakaraka (AmK): Career, wealth, ministers.
  - Bhratrukaraka (BK): Siblings, gurus.
  - Matrukaraka (MK): Mother, home.
  - Putrakaraka (PK): Children, intelligence.
  - Gnatikaraka (GK): Rivals, obstacles, diseases.
  - Darakaraka (DK): Spouse, partnerships.
- **Arudhas**: Image or reflection of a house.
  - Arudha Lagna (AL): How the world sees the native.
  - Upapada Lagna (UL): Marriage and relationships.
- **Karakamsa**: The sign occupied by the Atmakaraka in the Navamsha (D9).
- **Argala**: Intervening planetary influences based on specific houses from a sign.

## 2. Timing Systems
- **Chara Dasha**: Sign-based dasha system. Excellent for general life events.
- **Mandook Dasha**: Specialized Dasha used for certain charts or specific events (like battles/conflicts).
- **Narayana Dasha**: The supreme Dasha for all matters, showing the environment and general life progression.

## 3. Disagreements / Conflicts
- **7 vs 8 Chara Karakas**: K.N. Rao advocates for 7 Karakas (excluding Rahu). Sanjay Rath and P.V.R. Narasimha Rao advocate for 8 (including Rahu if two planets are at the exact same degree).
- **Calculation of Chara Dasha**: There are at least three different ways to calculate Chara Dasha (K.N. Rao method, Sanjay Rath method, classical method).

## 4. Extraction & Retrieval Directives
When retrieving Jaimini rules, the system MUST filter by `tradition: Jaimini`. The Evidence Engine must not apply Parashari aspects to Jaimini Yogas.
